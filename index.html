About me

My name is Jiaxun Huang. I am currently a senior Software Engineering student at the University of California, Irvine (UC Irvine).

I was born and raised in Guangzhou, China, and moved to San Francisco when I was 12. My grandmother and my aunt were both mathematics teachers in China, and their influence helped spark my interest in mathematics. This led me to study mathematics at City College of San Francisco (CCSF).

Although I enjoyed mathematics, I wanted to pursue a different path rather than become a teacher like them. When I applied to transfer, I chose Software Engineering at UC Irvine as a new direction to explore.

Coming from a mathematics background, I am still new to many topics in computer science and software engineering. I am learning as I go and discovering what interests me along the way. I am particularly interested in human-computer interaction (HCI), accessibility, and inclusive design.

Outside of School

Outside of school, I enjoy traveling, cooking, and listening to music. My cooking doesn't always turn out great, but I enjoy the process. It helps me relax and take a break from studying.
